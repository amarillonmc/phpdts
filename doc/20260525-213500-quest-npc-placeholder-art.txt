This change adds anime-style placeholder avatar assets for QUEST test NPCs and updates each NPC icon field to point at the new project-local assets.

  Normal headshot: 140x80, named quest*_*.jpg.
- 全身像：280x680，文件名追加_a后缀。
  Full-body portrait: 280x680, using the _a filename suffix.
